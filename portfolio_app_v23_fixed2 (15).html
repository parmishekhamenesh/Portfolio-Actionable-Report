A/E (%)StatusDescription
> 100%OverperformingSite generating more energy than expected. Can result from favorable weather, conservative modeling, or optimized performance. Positive but should be monitored for data accuracy.
60% – 100%On TargetSite performing within acceptable range of expectations. Energy generation is at or near projections, indicating healthy system operation.
< 60%UnderperformingSite generating significantly less energy than expected. May be caused by downtime, inverter issues, communication gaps, shading, soiling, or other system inefficiencies. Further investigation required.
General Definitions
TermDefinition
Site Inverter AvailabilityPercentage of time site inverters were online and capable of producing energy during the reporting period.
Performance RatioActual energy produced compared to expected energy based on weather conditions and system design.
Action FlagsIndicators highlighting urgent inverter or system-level issues, such as no production or low production events.
Underperforming SitesSites generating below defined performance thresholds (e.g., A/E% or availability below acceptable limits).
Alert SummaryOverview of active system alerts by type and duration, such as no production or communication failures.

General Definitions
TermDefinition
Site Inverter AvailabilityPercentage of time site inverters were online and capable of producing energy during the reporting period.
Performance Ratio (PR)Ratio of actual energy generated to expected energy, adjusted for weather conditions and system design. Formula: Actual kWh ÷ Expected kWh × 100. Primary KPI per IEC 61724-1.
Action FlagsIndicators highlighting urgent inverter or system-level issues, such as no production or low production events.
Underperforming SitesSites generating below defined performance thresholds (e.g., PR% or availability below acceptable limits).
Alert SummaryOverview of active system alerts by type and duration, such as no production or communication failures.
A/E% (Actual / Expected %)Ratio of actual energy generated to expected energy for the same period, expressed as a percentage. Also referred to as Performance Ratio (PR).
Final ScoreWeighted portfolio score used to assign the letter grade: Performance Ratio × 0.80 + Availability × 0.20.
EER (Energy Efficiency Ratio)Ratio of AC energy output to DC energy input. Measures inverter conversion efficiency, independent of weather conditions.
No CommunicationSite inverters are not communicating with the monitoring system. Generation data unavailable for the period.
No ProductionSite is online and communicating but generating zero or near-zero energy output.
